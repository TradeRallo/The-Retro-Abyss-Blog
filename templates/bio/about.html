About Retro Abyss

Retro Abyss is a personal creative project combining music, digital art, original characters, and worldbuilding into a single evolving universe.

The project follows its own fictional timeline, where music, artworks, characters, and events become parts of a larger story. Its chronology begins in 2003 and continues into the present.

Retro Abyss is constantly evolving, with each new work becoming another fragment of its world.

Status: Ongoing | Origin: 2008

Author

Saigotsuki Reiga is one of the central characters and the main mascot of Retro Abyss. Although he serves as a guide through the project and its world, Reiga is a fictional character rather than a direct representation of the author.

Philosophy

Retro Abyss is not just a blog — it is a timeline of evolution. Each entry, track, or artwork is a fragment of a larger narrative.

Imperfection is part of the process. Early works remain unchanged to preserve authenticity and the feeling of that moment in time.
